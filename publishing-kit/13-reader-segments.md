# Reader Segments and Promise Map

## Segment 1: Practitioner under delivery pressure
Pain:
- too much AI output, not enough control
- weak traceability
- fear of silent debt

Promise:
A-Dev gives this reader a way to move quickly without surrendering architecture, evidence, or recovery discipline.

## Segment 2: Senior engineer becoming orchestrator
Pain:
- feels reduced to copy/paste supervision
- lacks a shared operating model for agent work
- sees quality drift between prompts and outcomes

Promise:
A-Dev reframes the role from implementer-only to coordinator of a virtual delivery workflow.

## Segment 3: Small team lead with limited budget
Pain:
- cannot afford coordination-heavy process
- needs production-grade outcomes from thin capacity
- wants repeatability, not heroics

Promise:
A-Dev offers a lightweight but disciplined operating system for high-leverage delivery.

## Segment rule
Every public chapter, pitch, and talk should make it obvious which segment it serves and what concrete gain the reader gets.
