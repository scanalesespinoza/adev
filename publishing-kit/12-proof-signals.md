# Proof Signals and Packaging

This file turns A-Dev proof into reusable public signals.

## Core proof categories
| Category | Current signal | Stronger public packaging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operational proof | Homedir-derived guardrails and case studies | Public proof packet with selected incidents, decisions, and reusable lessons |
| Delivery rigor | PRs, CI, release flow, starter-kit rituals | Short evidence summaries that show how A-Dev closes loops under pressure |
| Reusability | Starter-kit templates and examples | Before/after adoption stories from new repos using the starter kit |
| Author credibility | Repo history and method language | Talks, workshops, essays, and testimonials tied to concrete outcomes |

## Minimum public proof packet
1. One evidence index page.
2. Three failure-derived case studies.
3. One starter-kit adoption example.
4. One summary showing how a principle changed after contact with reality.

## Packaging rule
Do not present proof as raw volume. Present it as curated signals that show method, rigor, and transferability.
