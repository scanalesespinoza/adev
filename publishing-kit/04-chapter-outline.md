# Chapter Outline (A-Dev)

- **00 Title / Front Matter / INDEX**  
  Promise, scope, TOC.

- **01 A-Dev Manifesto**  
  Superagency, 50/50, living baseline, simple machines. Outcome: mindset shift from hype to discipline.

- **02 From Team Member to Orchestrator**  
  The “ghost team” of AI roles; translating experience into constraints. Outcome: readers see their value in orchestration, not typing.

- **03 Atomic Iterations (1–2 hours)**  
  Definition → Implementation → Verification → Evidence; 24h marathon pattern; two-level commands (roadmap ↔ iteration). Outcome: run 1–2h cycles without drift.

- **04 Baseline / Rules of Engagement**  
  Non-negotiables: test everything, security-first, simple structure, traceability, stop the line. Live Alpha with flags; living baseline updates. Outcome: baseline as code-of-conduct for AI and humans.

- **05 The Digital Thread**  
  Plan → prompt → commit/tests; persona linkage; worked example (Google OAuth + health checks). Outcome: audit trail for proof of contribution.

- **06 Quality Density vs. Vanity Metrics**  
  Value per LOC, test/code ratio, complexity; Broad Quality Cycle (50/50); frugal architect stance. Outcome: measure what matters, avoid rework.

- **07 The Realistic Weekend Marathon**  
  Micro-sprints chained; Plan A only; rampage → steady state; 24h loop. Outcome: a weekend plan that ships.

- **08 HomeDir as Proof of Contribution**  
  Metrics: baseline adherence, iteration success, quality density, traceability, infra simplicity; public signals (CI/release). Outcome: build a profile that shows evidence.

- **09 Tough Objections**  
  AI did the work? hype? debt? security? Answers via traceability, automation, baseline discipline. Outcome: ready-made responses.

- **10 Closing**  
  Call to action and democratization note. Outcome: reader commits to one weekend feature with traceability.

- **Appendices**  
  Templates (iteration, prompt, foundation, health/deploy), glossary, checklists. Outcome: ready-to-use assets.
