# Book Proposal (A-Dev)

## Overview
Augmented Development (A-Dev) is the manual for the Architect-Orchestrator: ship systems you may not fully understand—but can fully validate—by eliminating coordination tax with baselines, flags, and the 50/50 quality rule. It is implementation-led (EvenFlow → HomeDir) and frugal (no heavy middleware, SSR-first, simple machines).

## Target Audience (segmented)
- **Solo developers / indie founders:** need production velocity with low overhead; pain: debt from AI haste; outcome: 1–2h loops with evidence.
- **Tech leads in SMEs/startups:** need predictable delivery with small teams; pain: QA gaps, regressions; outcome: 50/50 cycle + baseline to prevent rollbacks.
- **OSS maintainers:** need traceable, high-quality contributions; pain: noisy PRs; outcome: digital thread (plan → prompt → commit/tests) and templates.
- **DevRel/Community builders:** need real tasks gamified; pain: “eternal labs” of simulated work; outcome: Live Alpha with flags and Simple Machines.
- **Frustrated Visionary / Solo Product Engineer:** senior dev/architect who wants production-grade output with minimal coordination tax; values frugality and fast proof.

## Audience pains & outcomes
- Debt from AI-speed code → Living Baseline + Plan A only.
- Unpredictable releases → 50/50 Broad Quality Cycle and health checks.
- Regression risk → Digital Thread with persona linkage and evidence.
- Infra complexity → Simple Machines + feature flags (Live Alpha).
- Lack of proof → Case studies and CI/release badges as evidence.

## Need / Why Now
- 2026 crisis of AI-driven debt: speed without discipline breaks products.
- Teams are smaller; individuals must operate at “team” scale (superagency).
- Demand for practical, verifiable workflows (plan → prompt → commit/tests) with AI assistants.
