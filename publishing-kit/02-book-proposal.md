# Book Proposal (A-Dev)

## Overview
Augmented Development (A-Dev) is the manual for the practitioner-orchestrator: ship systems you may not fully understand, but can fully validate, by reducing coordination tax with baselines, flags, and a disciplined create/verify loop. It is implementation-led and frugal: keep the stack simple, keep the workflow explicit, and keep the evidence visible.

## Target Audience (segmented)
- **Individual contributors:** need production velocity with low overhead; pain: debt from AI haste; outcome: short loops with evidence.
- **Team leads and architects:** need predictable delivery with small teams; pain: QA gaps and regressions; outcome: a repeatable quality cycle and a living baseline.
- **OSS maintainers:** need traceable, high-quality contributions; pain: noisy PRs; outcome: a digital thread from plan to tests and review notes.
- **Platform and DevEx teams:** need workflows that stay portable across tools; pain: tool lock-in and fragmented practices; outcome: a vendor-neutral baseline and reusable templates.
- **Operational owners:** need proof that changes can be shipped and rolled back safely; pain: unclear release behavior; outcome: flags, checks, and explicit verification.

## Audience pains & outcomes
- Debt from AI-speed code -> living baseline + one clear path.
- Unpredictable releases -> quality cycle and health checks.
- Regression risk -> digital thread with evidence.
- Infra complexity -> simple machines + feature flags.
- Lack of proof -> case studies and CI/release badges as evidence.

## Need / Why Now
- Speed without discipline breaks products.
- Smaller teams and individual contributors are expected to operate at higher leverage.
- Demand is growing for practical, verifiable workflows (plan -> prompt -> commit/tests) with AI assistants.
