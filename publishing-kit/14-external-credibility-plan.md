# External Credibility Plan

## Objective
Strengthen authority without diluting the framework into hype or generic AI commentary.

## Credibility assets to build
1. Public talks or live walkthroughs anchored in one concrete case study.
2. Testimonials from practitioners who adopted a starter-kit ritual or A-Dev flow.
3. Short essays that show principle -> evidence -> lesson.
4. Download and usage signals for the starter kit, when available.

## Collection rules
- Prefer specific outcomes over praise.
- Capture what changed in behavior, not only that the material felt useful.
- Keep every proof claim traceable to a repo asset, talk, workshop, or adoption artifact.

## Near-term moves
- Turn stage 1 and stage 2 assets into one shareable walkthrough.
- Ask early readers for feedback on clarity, transferability, and trust.
- Track which starter-kit files are adopted or reused most often.
