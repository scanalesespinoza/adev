This assessment uses ADEV repository evidence from the [current inventory](00-inventory.md). Scores are editorial judgments, not market validation. Homedir-derived operational claims require direct links to the canonical [Homedir repository](https://github.com/scanalesespinoza/homedir); the presence or count of ADEV case-study files does not independently validate their contents.

**Average:** 3.1/5 (25/40)

**Recommendation:** Not submission-ready. The next work should improve canonical-source clarity, comparable-title metadata, chapter outcomes, and evidence quality before making market claims.

## Priority fixes

1. Reconcile or explicitly retire the historical `manuscript/` surface as a competing editorial source.
2. Complete publication metadata and add verified, relevant AI-era comparable titles.
3. Add one explicit reader outcome to each chapter introduction.
4. Link material claims and reference cases directly to the relevant Homedir repository artifact; label unsupported or non-Homedir material explicitly.
5. Replace aspirational adoption numbers with a measurement plan that names source, owner, cadence, and observation date.
6. Collect external proof only when it exists; do not publish placeholders as evidence.
