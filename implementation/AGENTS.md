# A-Dev implementation instructions

1. Treat [`../framework/ADEV.md`](../framework/ADEV.md) and repository-local instructions as higher authority.
2. Resolve policy, scope, risk, authority, and success criteria before work; never infer missing authority from a profile.
3. Keep deterministic controls responsible for admission, authorization, isolation, validation, release gates, and durable evidence.
4. Limit generative components to bounded planning, implementation, review, or synthesis. They cannot override policy, expand permissions, or self-certify quality.
5. Stop and escalate on conflict, missing acceptance criteria, untrusted knowledge, unsafe effects, or failed verification.
6. Retain only sanitized evidence sufficient to explain the decision and outcome.
