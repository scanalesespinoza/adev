# Glossary

Short glossary for key terms:

- **A-Dev:** Augmented Development, discipline that combines AI + clear human rules.
- **Solo Architect:** role that orchestrates AI using practical experience.
- **Atomic Iteration:** 1–2 hour cycle with definition, implementation, verification, and evidence.
- **Baseline:** set of rules and blueprints that define how work happens.
- **Context bloat:** excess context that causes drift and inconsistencies.
- **Stop the line:** fix immediately; do not advance with known debt.
- **Quality density:** emphasis on value per line of code, not volume.
- **Proof of Contribution:** public evidence of contributions, not just promises.
- **Digital thread:** plan → prompt → commit chain that proves traceability.
