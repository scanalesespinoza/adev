# Overlay: marketing automation

**Applies when** a system drafts, schedules, or publishes marketing or social content on behalf of a product or person.

1. Use a staged rollout: internal drafts, then controlled approval or scheduling, then autopublishing only after production validation.
2. Use only real and verifiable product data. Never invent numbers, milestones, or claims.
3. Validate drafts and claims internally before linking external channels. Keep higher-risk networks in explicit-approval mode until sustained quality is proven.
4. Before enabling any production scheduler, every external publishing or automation channel has secrets managed outside the repository, deduplication, per-channel rate limiting, and a global kill switch.
