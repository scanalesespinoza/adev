# A-Dev Manifesto

## Core idea
A-Dev is about **giving people superpowers**, not replacing them. AI is the engine; the human remains the Architect, QA, and Quality Officer.

## Myth vs. reality
- **Myth:** "AI writes your company in a weekend."
- **Reality:** AI accelerates, but the value comes from how you direct, validate, and uphold the baseline.

## Honest timeline
- **One weekend:** one production-ready feature.
- **1 month:** an integrated MVP.
- **6 months:** a serious platform, if you keep discipline and the baseline.

## Core phrase
"I am not selling magical speed; I am demonstrating discipline + real efficiency."

## Motivation
This is about democratization: people without a big-company budget can still build enterprise-grade systems. The resources now exist; the differentiator is the discipline to use them well.
