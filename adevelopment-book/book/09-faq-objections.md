# Tough objections (and honest answers)

## What if AI did the work?
Highlight traceability: prompts, decisions, and commits show your role as Architect/QA.

## Isn’t this replacing people?
No. The same people produce more. A-Dev is about giving practitioners superpowers, not removing them.

## Isn’t it hype if your platform is still under construction?
Transparency is the defense: show the baseline, iteration traces, and quality metrics.

## How do you avoid technical debt?
Short loops + baseline + "stop the line" when debt appears. Fix it in the same iteration.

## How do you prove security/quality without a big team?
Automate checks, require test evidence, and keep the plan → prompt → commit chain. Discipline substitutes headcount.

---
**Key takeaway:** Objections are answered with evidence: traceability, baseline discipline, and automated checks.

**Apply in 20 minutes:** Draft a 3-line response to your toughest stakeholder question and include the specific proof (commit/CI link) you will show.
