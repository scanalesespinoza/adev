# From team member to orchestrator

## The psychological shift

The leap is not only technical: you move from **typing** to **directing**. You stop measuring value by lines written and start measuring by how you orchestrate the system.

## The differentiator

Your hands-on experience across the lifecycle (dev, QA, security, ops, PM) becomes the raw material to steer AI effectively.

## Strongest point

The highest-value human task is **translating real experience into constraints**—rules, limits, and standards that keep coherence while AI executes.
