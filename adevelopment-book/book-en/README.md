# ADEV — English adaptation of v2.0.0

This directory is the working editorial home for *ADEV: The Practice of AI Agentic Development*, the English adaptation of the consolidated Spanish `v2.0.0` edition.

## Source canon

The source is `adevelopment-book/book-es/` as published under tag `v2.0.0` at commit `061425611c19a55faea90bdcd94bf99e5beb4894`. The English text is an adaptation for professional fluency, not a mechanical translation. It may change sentence structure and cultural framing, but it must preserve meaning, evidence boundaries, technical relationships, and author responsibility.

## Status

Editorial mapping is complete. Translation has not yet passed the completion gate. Files in this directory must not be presented as a released English edition until the full manifest, audits, PDF, EPUB, and author approval are complete.

## Working rules

- Follow the [English adaptation plan](../../publishing-kit/20-english-v2-adaptation-plan.md).
- Use `ADEV`, not the historical expansion *Augmented Development*.
- Keep the Spanish `v2.0.0` manuscript unchanged while adapting English.
- Preserve direct evidence links and uncertainty language.
- Review terminology across completed chapters after every part.
- Keep technical assets canonical in English and link to them rather than duplicating doctrine.

## Historical English manuscript
