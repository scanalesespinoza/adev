# A-Dev Book and Collateral

This directory contains a concise book on Augmented Development (A-Dev) and a kit of collateral ready to share across formats.

## Book
Chapters in `book/` follow a brief narrative flow:
1. `00-title-page.md`: author, contact, version, and tagline.
2. `00-front-matter.md`: purpose, audience, and the honest promise.
3. `INDEX.md`: quick navigation and one-line takeaways.
4. `01-adev-manifesto.md`: the A-Dev Manifesto.
5. `02-solo-architect.md`: from team player to orchestrator.
6. `03-atomic-iterations.md`: 1–2 hour atomic iterations.
7. `04-baseline-rules-of-engagement.md`: baseline and rules of engagement.
8. `05-traceability-digital-thread.md`: the digital thread of traceability.
9. `06-quality-density-metrics.md`: quality-density metrics that matter.
10. `07-weekend-marathon-playbook.md`: a realistic weekend playbook.
11. `08-homedir-vision.md`: HomeDir as proof of contribution.
12. `09-faq-objections.md`: tough objections with candid answers.
13. `10-closing.md`: closing and call to action.

Practical appendices in `book/appendices/` include templates, glossary, and checklists.

## Reusable collateral
`collateral/` holds ready-to-use materials for one-pagers, talk outlines, speaker notes, video series, and LinkedIn posts.

## Editorial summary
Augmented Development is how one person, with real experience and clear rules, can orchestrate an AI team to deliver professional software with short cycles, evidence, and quality.
