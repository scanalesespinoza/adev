# A-Dev Book and Collateral

This directory contains a concise book on Augmented Development (A-Dev) and a kit of collateral ready to share across formats.

## Book

Chapters in `book/` follow a brief narrative flow:

1. `00-front-matter.md`: purpose, audience, and the honest promise.
2. `01-manifiesto-adev.md`: the A-Dev Manifesto.
3. `02-el-solo-architect.md`: from team player to orchestrator.
4. `03-atomic-iterations.md`: 1–2 hour atomic iterations.
5. `04-baseline-rules-of-engagement.md`: baseline and rules of engagement.
6. `05-traceability-digital-thread.md`: the digital thread of traceability.
7. `06-quality-density-metrics.md`: quality-density metrics that matter.
8. `07-weekend-marathon-playbook.md`: a realistic weekend playbook.
9. `08-homedir-vision.md`: HomeDir as proof of contribution.
10. `09-faq-objections.md`: tough objections with candid answers.
11. `10-closing.md`: closing and call to action.

Practical appendices in `book/appendices/` include templates, glossary, and checklists.

## Reusable collateral

`collateral/` holds ready-to-use materials for one-pagers, talk outlines, speaker notes, video series, and LinkedIn posts.

## Editorial summary

Augmented Development is how one person, with real experience and clear rules, can orchestrate an AI team to deliver professional software with short cycles, evidence, and quality.
