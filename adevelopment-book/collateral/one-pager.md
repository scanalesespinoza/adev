# A-Dev in one page

## What A-Dev is

Augmented Development: a discipline for orchestrating AI with human experience and clear rules.

## Why now

AI accelerates execution but needs direction, a baseline, and traceability to deliver real value.

## How it looks in practice

- **Atomic Iterations:** 1–2 hour cycles with definition, implementation, verification, and evidence.
- **Baseline:** non-negotiable rules for security, testing, and structure.

## Realistic outcomes

- **Weekend:** one production-ready feature.
- **1 month:** integrated MVP.
- **6 months:** serious platform with sustained discipline.

## Closing phrase

Your integrity promise: "discipline + real efficiency," not magical speed.
