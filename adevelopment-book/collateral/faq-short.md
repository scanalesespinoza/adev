# Short FAQ

- **Can AI do everything?** No. AI is the engine; you define constraints, validate, and close the loop with evidence.
- **What can I achieve in a weekend?** One production-ready feature, with tests and traceability.
- **How do I avoid hype if the platform is still evolving?** Publish the baseline, iterations, and metrics; transparency is the defense.
- **How do I handle security and quality without a big team?** Automate checks, apply simple rules, and fix immediately (stop the line).
