# ADEV — primera edición en español

This directory is the editorial source of truth for the first edition of *ADEV: La práctica de AI Agentic Development*.

The manuscript is written in Spanish to preserve the author's native voice, Latin American experience, and conceptual precision. The framework and technical assets remain canonical in English so they can be reused across tools, teams, and industries. A future English edition should be adapted from the completed Spanish manuscript rather than translated mechanically from the historical English draft.

## Status

Developmental drafting is complete. [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) defines the implemented narrative architecture, and every target in [`Book.txt`](Book.txt) exists. The manuscript is now entering copyediting and publication-system validation; this status does not mean that a release PDF or EPUB is ready.

## Editorial rules

- Lead with human problems and observable outcomes; introduce technology only when it clarifies the practice.
- Preserve the author's voice without publishing promotional absolutes as facts.
- Distinguish observed evidence, author experience, interpretation, proposal, and open gap.
- Keep Homedir and EventFlow as proving grounds; extract vendor-neutral and industry-neutral lessons.
- Connect every practice chapter to an applicable framework or starter-kit asset.
- Use `ADEV` for the practice and `ADEV.md` for the repository entry point.
- Treat `AI Agentic Development` as the canonical expansion of ADEV for this edition.
- Do not use commit counts, lines of code, elapsed time, cost savings, or ROI as proof of value without a reproducible method and a valid comparison.

## Build order

[`Book.txt`](Book.txt) is the Spanish first-edition manifest. All listed sources exist. The production workflow still targets the historical English manuscript and must migrate to this manifest in a dedicated, reversible iteration with generated-artifact validation.
